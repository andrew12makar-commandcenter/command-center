## What this says about the lead engine

The government-first map and Overture snapshot are useful for *finding names and addresses*, but they do not establish online need. A map row with no URL can hide a real website, while a recorded website can be broken, stale, or point to the wrong branch. The live checks found actionable cases by opening the exact Google profile and following its website link, not by trusting source URL presence or absence.

The product’s current warnings already say that a missing Overture URL is only a research clue. The next useful workflow improvement is a **read-only public-presence review card** before qualification: save the exact Maps URL, current website URL, captured time, address/phone match, specific observable issue, and a source screenshot/note.
